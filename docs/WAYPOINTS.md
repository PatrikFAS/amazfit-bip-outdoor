# Navigator waypoint collection

The six slots are HOME, CAR, CAMP, BOAT, POINT 1 and POINT 2.

- Navigator opens on the distance screen. Swipe right for the **Legend** and left for the **Compass**. Tap anywhere in the **WAYPOINTS** row to browse saved points. Reverse the swipe or tap **BACK** to return to distance. Press the side button to exit from any screen.
- Compass shows the watch heading and a larger green direction arrow when a waypoint bearing is available. Red **N** and yellow GPS track stay around the edge. **RAW** indicates the firmware heading with variation Off. With the correct local variation, the heading is shown as **T** for true. Compass heading and north are available before saving a waypoint.

- **WAYPOINTS** opens the active slot; if no point has been saved, it opens HOME. **PREV** and **NEXT** browse all six slots, including empty ones. **BACK** returns to the page that opened the slot browser.
- On an empty slot, **SAVE HERE** records the current position and **COORDS** starts coordinate entry. The first saved point becomes the active target. Both actions occupy full-width rows. Saving from GPS requires a valid fix; if one is unavailable, the point is not saved.
- On an occupied slot, **SELECT** makes an inactive point the active target and returns to Navigator. **OPTIONS** is available for any saved point and contains separate full-width rows for **REPLACE HERE**, **COORDS**, and **DELETE**. Replacing from GPS requires a valid fix and a second confirmation tap. **DELETE** also requires a second confirmation tap. Changing slots, leaving the page or using another action cancels a pending confirmation.
- Coordinate entry does not require GPS. Enter latitude, then longitude, using the large numeric keypad. Tap **N/S** or **E/W** to choose the hemisphere; defaults are north and east. Coordinates are decimal degrees with up to five decimal places. **DEL** erases the last character. **NEXT** advances from latitude to longitude and then to review. The review shows the slot and signed coordinates. Tap **SAVE POINT** or **REPLACE POINT** to commit. **BACK** or a right swipe steps back and cancels coordinate entry as before.
- Navigator starts GPS when opened and keeps it active across Navigator, Legend, Compass, and the waypoint screens. It stops GPS when exited. Use **Diagnostics → GPS** for the manual GPS toggle. During a brief gap in readings, Navigator keeps the last accepted distance and bearing and shows yellow **LAST FIX** for about 10 seconds. A longer gap clears those readings and returns to **WAITING FOR FIX**. GPS waypoint writes pause after about 30 seconds of unchanged coordinates. With a compass heading, **LAST FIX** can retain the last distance and bearing for up to one minute after the last coordinate change, then shows **FIX UNCHANGED**.
- Tap **M/KM** or **M/NM** to change units. Both modes show metres below 1,000 m. The next waypoint write retains the units choice. The side button exits the app and requests sensor shutdown.
- The red **N** shows north using the current compass reference. The green arrow shows the waypoint direction. The yellow arrow shows GPS course over ground after fixes are about 15 m apart. Yellow continues to show your direction of travel if you walk away from the waypoint. All three share the screen perimeter. The arrows tilt toward their bearings and shift along the edge if needed to keep each marker visible.
- Open **WAYPOINTS → SET** to adjust **VARIATION** with **−** and **+** in one-degree steps. The display shows east or west. **RESET OFF** clears the draft and **SAVE** applies it. **BACK** discards edits. Variation defaults to Off and applies to Navigator across all slots. Apply a local correction if the firmware heading is magnetic. The numeric **BRG … T** already uses true north. See [Compass testing](COMPASS-CHECK.md).

## Storage and compatibility

`src/waypoints.c` stores a 114-byte collection: BIPC magic, format version, selected slot, occupied mask, units, variation in version 2, six existing 17-byte BIPW coordinate records, and outer CRC32. Unoccupied slots have zero bytes. Decoding validates lengths, fields, CRCs and coordinates before changing the caller's state. Version 1 collections retain their slots, active selection and units with variation Off. Legacy single-waypoint BIPW records migrate into HOME; 114-byte zero-padded legacy records are also accepted.

Writes use the BipOS settings API and verify the exact bytes generated by the encoder before the UI confirms success. Failed save/select/delete leaves the running collection unchanged. This is corruption detection and verified writing, not an atomic transaction across power loss. No raw flash addresses are used.

A RES replacement may reset settings before this migration can run. Record any valuable coordinates separately before installing. The preceding single-waypoint build was confirmed to survive a watch restart; the new collection requires another hardware test. The host harness tests storage failures, migration, slot changes and drawing calls, but cannot establish physical flash behaviour or BipOS loader allocation.

## Watch validation

1. To verify coordinate entry without waiting for GPS, open Navigator and tap **WAYPOINTS**. Confirm the empty collection opens HOME, then choose **COORDS**. Enter latitude `49.28273` with **N**, longitude `123.12074` with **W**, and advance to review. Confirm the displayed signed coordinates are `+49.28273, -123.12074`, then save. Confirm HOME is active and **BACK** returns to Navigator.
2. Save another point with **COORDS**, for example `0.12345` **S**, `0.54321` **E**. Confirm the review shows `-0.12345, +0.54321`. Check that **DEL** erases the last character, hemisphere controls switch signs, and **BACK** and right swipe step back and cancel without saving. Use **PREV/NEXT** to find the point, verify **SELECT** activates an inactive point, then open **OPTIONS** on the active point. Confirm replacement and deletion each need a second tap. Also verify **COORDS** replacement updates that slot after review.
3. For GPS saving and return guidance, open Navigator outside and wait for **FIX READY**. Open **WAYPOINTS**, select an empty slot, and use **SAVE HERE** to mark the trailhead. Confirm a missing/invalid fix prevents saving or replacing. Walk a short known distance and stop briefly: check whether yellow **LAST FIX** retains the last distance and bearing during a short reading gap, then changes to **WAITING FOR FIX** after roughly 10 seconds without readings. Resume movement and confirm readings return. Walk back using the large distance and numeric true bearing; within a displayed 10 m, **NEAR TARGET** replaces the bearing.
4. While Navigator is open, swipe from the main screen to Legend and Compass. Check that the fix is retained and only the side button exits to Applications. Then move between **WAYPOINTS**, **OPTIONS**, and coordinate entry. Confirm GPS remains active and that the last-fix display behaves consistently across pages. Remain stationary for about 30 seconds and check that GPS save/replace pauses until coordinates change. Check daylight readability and test the manual toggle in **Diagnostics → GPS**.
5. Exit, restart the watch, and verify both entered points and the selected target persist. Confirm Navigator exits with the side button.
